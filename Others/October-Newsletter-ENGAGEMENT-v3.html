My take

October is showing us that even with higher mortgage rates, buyers are still competing for the right homes. Oakland continues to offer opportunities depending on the neighborhood and condition, while San Francisco remains especially competitive. For buyers, being prepared and knowing when to negotiate matters. For sellers, pricing and presentation are still key. The market is active, but buyers are paying close attention to value.

Bay Area Cities Ranked the Greenest in America!

Here's something pretty cool about the Bay Area. According to WalletHub's 2026 rankings, Oakland, San Jose, and San Francisco took the top three spots among the 100 largest U.S. cities for environmental sustainability. Fremont also made the top 10, coming in at number seven!

#1
Oakland ranked #1, standing out for its air quality, low greenhouse gas emissions, parks, and access to bike paths.
#2
San Jose came in #2, thanks in part to its solar energy use, with more than 50% of its electricity coming from renewable sources.
#3
San Francisco ranked #3, leading the country in community gardens and local food programs while also standing out for water conservation and public transportation.
 

The rankings looked at everything from air quality and renewable energy to transportation and green spaces. It's another reminder of what makes living in the Bay Area special, beyond just the homes and neighborhoods.
